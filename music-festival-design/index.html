Fun Fun Fun Fest Branding

Fun Fun Fun Fest is a multi-genre music festival in Austin, Texas, featuring hip-hop, indie rock, punk, and comedy. I created a brand identity centered around a 3D isometric stair typography logo, where the ascending steps visually represent the growing sense of fun and energy that builds throughout the festival. The vibrant gradient colors reflect the festival's diverse, high-energy atmosphere. The system includes festival banners, tickets, lanyards for different access levels, t-shirts, posters, skateboards, and hats.
