Kinetic Typography

This project is a kinetic typography piece that synchronizes animated type with the audio track, using motion to express the song's meaning, mood, and rhythm. I used an aurora as the background to reflect the mysterious atmosphere of the music video, while the motion of the type brings the song's lyrics to life.
