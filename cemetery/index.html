Cemetery

Cemetery is a display typeface designed for headlines and short to medium-length text, available in a single Thin weight. Inspired by the ornate wrought iron fences of New York, it merges delicate long pointed serifs with Victorian-era scrollwork to evoke a sense of melancholic beauty and dark elegance. Ideal for music album covers, film titles, posters, and luxury branding — best used at 72pt and above.
