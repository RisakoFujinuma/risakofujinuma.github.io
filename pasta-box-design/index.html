DE CECCO
Pasta Package

A letterpress-printed pasta packaging for De Cecco, designed for two box sizes — Fusilli and Linguine. Inspired by the warmth of home cooking, the design features a quatrefoil pattern in wheat-yellow, referencing the main ingredient of pasta, combined with navy blue typography and orange and green accents. Both boxes share a unified visual identity, printed flat on 11×17" paper and folded into shape.
